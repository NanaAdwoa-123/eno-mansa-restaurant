Our Story & Kitchen

Warm Ashanti Hospitality on Osei Tutu I Avenue

At Eno Mansa Restaurant, every pot is seasoned with care and time-honored Ghanaian recipes. Whether you are stepping out on a busy afternoon lunch break in central Kumasi or picking up a hearty meal for the family, we believe real food should taste just like home.

From freshly pounded fufu and charcoal-grilled Volta tilapia to rich kontomire stew and spiced sobolo, we source fresh tomatoes, garden eggs, yam, plantain, and spices daily so every plate arrives hot, generous, and deeply satisfying.

Fresh Morning Prep

Soups, stews, and waakye are cooked fresh each morning for peak daytime flavor.

Generous Portions

Honest, filling servings paired with tender proteins and homemade shito.

Central Kumasi Access
